# Artes pixel art dos desafios

Geradas com ImageGen integrado e publicadas no S3, com referências persistentes nos campos `arcade_game.image` e `challenge.image`. Os PNGs em `frontend/public/challenges/` continuam como fallback para instalações ainda não migradas. O resolver `frontend/src/lib/challengeArtwork.ts` substitui placeholders locais e mantém URLs da API. O card do Mapa de Treinamento usa `worldmap-pixel.png`; a arte do mapa dentro do jogo permanece intacta. Consulte `media-storage.md` para publicação e cuidados com URLs assinadas.

## Prompts utilizados

### worldmap-pixel.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone pixel art game illustration for a children's cybersecurity academy UI, displayed at 100px, matching an existing icon set of purple phishing hook with cream envelope, teal folders with lock, and purple teal word tiles. Subject: a charming folded cream parchment adventure map, tilted slightly, with a simple dotted purple route linking a teal starting marker to a small golden star destination, and a little lavender flag rising from the map. Style: playful restrained 16-bit pixel art, large chunky square pixels, crisp dark purple stepped outlines, simple broad shapes, subtle pixel shading, limited purple lavender cream teal golden palette. Centered compact composition filling 80 percent of a square canvas with safe margins on ALL sides. A few tiny scattered teal coral and purple square confetti pixels near the subject, sparse. True transparent alpha background, NOT white, NOT checkerboard, no ground plane, no shadow rectangle, no background or frame. No text or labels. Keep simple and legible, not a realistic terrain landscape, no tiny detailed mountains or castles, no photorealism, no smooth vector look.
